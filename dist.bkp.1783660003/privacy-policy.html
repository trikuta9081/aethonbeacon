Aethon Beacon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Summary

Aethon Beacon is designed to work local-first. In the current release, most user data stays on the user's device unless the user exports it or later chooses a sync feature.

Information Stored On Your Device

The following categories are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is transmitted unless you explicitly share, export, or (for verification) enter your phone or email:

  • Check-ins and journal entries
  • Gratitude journal entries
  • Selected identity profile and role
  • Reminder preferences (including a custom time you choose)
  • Theme preference (system/light/dark) and personal accent color
  • SOS number and trusted contacts
  • Issue guide progress and redress preferences
  • Community drafts, saved items, moderation state, and verification status
  • Support locality for nearby help searches
  • Saved birth chart(s) — birth date, time, and place used only to compute your Rashi, Nakshatra, Lagna, and Vimshottari Dasha locally
  • Named chart profiles (self, family, partner) if you save them
  • Mantra favorites
  • Journal PIN (a 4-digit number you choose to lock your journal history)
  • Case notes and case record (only if you use the Reporting Companion) — including your chosen category of harm, incident dates, complaint dates, FIR number, officer name, and any private notes you type. This information does NOT leave your device unless you export it.

Explicitly not stored: we do not store any biometric fingerprint or face-recognition data; the journal PIN is stored as-is on-device only and is not transmitted anywhere.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to show the user's own history, tailor guidance, keep reminders and support paths working, enable SOS and trusted-contact actions, and gate community chat until phone and email verification are complete.

Sharing

The app does not need a social account to function in its current local-first form. Data remains on the device unless the user exports it or a future sync feature is explicitly enabled.

Safety and Children

Aethon Beacon includes SOS actions, trusted contacts, official helplines, and complaint redress paths. Children, students, and vulnerable users should use the app with appropriate supervision where required.

Retention and Changes

Local data stays on the device until the user clears it, exports it, or changes feature settings. This policy may be updated as the product grows.

Users can request deletion of online verification, AI Help, or community support records through the Aethon Beacon data deletion page.
