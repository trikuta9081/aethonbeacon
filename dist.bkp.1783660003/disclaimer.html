Safety Disclaimer

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Purpose

Aethon Beacon is a clarity, guidance, support, and redress-preparation tool. It is not an emergency service, medical service, legal service, counselling clinic, government authority, or substitute for professional judgment.

Emergency and Crisis Use

If the user is in immediate danger, facing self-harm risk, violence, medical distress, abuse, harassment, criminal threat, or any grave situation, the user should contact emergency services, local authorities, trusted contacts, nearby hospitals, official helplines, or qualified professionals first.

Professional Help

The app can guide users toward psychologists, counsellors, doctors, hospitals, official portals, and professional directories. Users should verify qualifications, availability, fees, locality, and suitability before relying on any provider.

Complaint and Redress Guidance

Complaint guidance (including the in-app Reporting Companion for abuse, harassment, and assault) helps users prepare facts, evidence, office routes, escalation steps, and follow-up. It cannot guarantee that any institution, officer, authority, private entity, or platform will accept, investigate, resolve, or respond within a particular time. Statutory references (POSH Act 2013, PWDVA 2005, POCSO 2012, IPC, BNS, CrPC, IT Act, and the Lalita Kumari v. State of U.P. (2014) judgment) are provided as general orientation, not legal opinion. Users are strongly encouraged to consult a qualified advocate, the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA — dial 15100 in India), or a legal aid clinic before relying on any interpretation.

Counselling Content and the Multi-Dimensional Reading Engine

The Deep Reading, CBT reframe, Jungian mirror, Vedic lens, chakra guidance, mantra library, Vimshottari Dasha, and daily horoscope features are self-reflection aids drawing on traditional wisdom and general psychology principles. They are NOT a substitute for diagnosis, therapy, medication, medical care, or emergency response. If any content feels triggering, discontinue and use a helpline or a qualified professional. Aethon Beacon does not diagnose, prescribe, or treat any condition.

Astrology and Traditional Practices

Vedic astrological outputs (Rashi, Nakshatra, Lagna, Dasha, Panchang, Chogadiya, Ekadashi calendar, compatibility) are computed with widely-used approximations (Lahiri Ayanamsa, Meeus formulae) and are intended for spiritual reflection and cultural continuity. They are not scientific predictions. Life decisions should not be based on astrological output alone.

Community and Children

Community chat and messaging are intended for verified users only. Children, students, and vulnerable users should use the app with appropriate supervision where required.
