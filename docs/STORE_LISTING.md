## Full description

**NAYIQ is a calm, private companion for the hard parts of life — and a
practical toolkit for getting real help when you need it.**

Whether you're anxious, stuck, grieving, or just want to think something through,
NAYIQ listens, reflects back what it hears, and gives you a personalised
path forward — not generic advice. And when a problem is bigger than a feeling —
a complaint, a threat, an unfair situation — it hands you the exact next step,
the right office, and the words to use.

**A private counselling engine**
A two-way, adaptive conversation that asks the right questions and builds a
personalised reflection and journey for your situation. It quietly draws on your
own check-in history and mood trend, and never fabricates or judges. Replies can
be read aloud.

**Calming tools that actually help**
A Pristine Tone Engine with binaural, solfège, isochronic, bilateral, and
ambient sounds — with a built-in hearing-safety limiter, session presets, and a
background mini-player. Plus breathing guides, a reset timer, and guided
meditation matched to what you're working on.

**Help & Redress**
Emergency SOS, verified helplines, and complaint tools: templates, first-script
wording, evidence checklists, and the right first office and escalation path for
your situation.

**A plain-language birth chart (Vedic Jyotish)**
Real sidereal astronomy — not a generic daily horoscope. Get a multi-dimensional
Moon-chart reading with clear scores, explanations, and practical remedies; your
Dasha timeline and a 15-year forecast; classical Yogas, Ashtakavarga, and
Shadbala; and an **Ask the chart** feature for any question. Fully available in
**English and Hindi**.

**Patterns & journaling**
Private local journaling and check-ins reveal your weekly and monthly trends and
a supportive progress report.

**Built around your privacy**
Your journal and check-ins stay on your device by default. No cross-app
tracking. Community and verification are opt-in.

NAYIQ is a supportive wellbeing tool, not a medical device or a
substitute for professional or emergency care.
