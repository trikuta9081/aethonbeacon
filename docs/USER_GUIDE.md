# Aethon Beacon — User Guide

Welcome to Aethon Beacon. This guide walks through every screen and how to use
it. Aethon Beacon works fully offline for the core features; your journal and
check-ins stay on your device unless you choose to verify and connect.

---

## First run

When you open the app the first time, a short setup asks you to:

1. **Choose an identity** (student, professional, parent, caregiver, officer,
   and others). This tailors the tone and the recommended support paths.
2. **Set a reminder cadence** (optional daily check-in reminders).
3. **Set an emergency (SOS) number** for your region.

You can replay this setup any time from **Settings → Show onboarding again**.

---

## The main tabs

### Today
Your home base. Shows your streak, a quick way to say what's on your mind, and
one-tap actions. The **SOS** button calls your configured emergency number.

### Journal
Guided check-ins. Write a line about how you're doing; the app turns it into a
timestamped entry and uses it (privately) to understand your mood trend over
time. Your history lives here.

### Meditation & Calm
Breathing guides, a reset timer, and situation-matched routines. If you have an
active focus issue, a matched practice (a specific tone + breath pattern) is
suggested at the top.

### Tones
A library of therapeutic sounds — binaural beats, solfège frequencies,
isochronic tones, bilateral audio, and ambient soundscapes, plus curated
"Healing Programs" for your issue. Controls:

- **Play once** / **Loop** / **Pause** for the selected tone.
- **Session presets** (quick reset, deep calm, sleep, focus) and a timer.
- **Safe gain** control — the engine has a built-in limiter to protect hearing.
- A **mini-player** keeps audio going when you leave the Tones tab, like a music
  app. Tap it to pause/resume/stop from anywhere.

### Path (Guide)
Structured, issue-specific guidance: a principle, a practice, and a boundary for
what you're working on. It also surfaces a multidimensional Moon-chart layer and
a **guided journey** that walks you across the right tabs step by step. If an
issue has come up before, you'll see a gentle note acknowledging the pattern.

### Ask / Counselling
A private, two-way counselling chat. Tell it what's going on; it asks a few
adaptive questions, then gives you a personalised reflection and a recommended
journey — never generic advice. Replies can be read aloud (toggle the speaker in
the header). If you've journalled or checked in recently, it takes that into
account. The **Scope and safety** note at the top can be collapsed once read.

### Help & Redress
For when something is wrong and you need real-world help:

- **SOS** and important helpline numbers, one tap away.
- Directories: legal aid, government grievance cells, police/officers, health
  and hospitals, mental-health lines.
- Complaint **templates**, first-script wording, evidence checklists, and a
  recommended first office + escalation path for your situation.

### Vedic
Your birth chart, read in plain language. Enter your exact date, time, and place
of birth to unlock:

- A **plain-language chart brief** (house-by-house).
- The **multi-dimensional Moon-chart** reading — a score, verdict, explanation, and a
  practical remedy for 48 areas of life, with a full calculation trace.
- Your **Dasha timeline** and a **15-year forecast**.
- Classical layers: Yogas, Ashtakavarga, and Shadbala strengths.
- **Ask the chart** — type any question (career, relationship, health, money,
  timing) and get a calculated answer with a remedy.

Use the **EN / हिं** toggle to switch the whole Vedic reading and Ask-the-chart
between English and Hindi.

### Patterns (Insights)
Your weekly and monthly trend, a pattern critique in your identity's voice, and
a progress report card. It also flags when more check-ins would sharpen the read.

### Community
A moderated support feed and verified private chat. Chat and messaging unlock
after you complete one verification (phone or email). You can react, save,
report, and hide items; offensive/adult content is blocked.

### Explore (Search)
Global search across guides, redress routes, official help, professional help,
and community notes, with quick shortcuts.

### Settings / Profile / Language
Change your identity, emergency number, and reminders; export a local backup;
switch the app language; and manage sign-in. Admin controls are separate and
gated.

## Voice features

- **Read aloud:** guidance and counselling replies can be spoken. Mute from the
  speaker button inside the chat or in Settings.
- **Voice input:** where supported, tap the mic to speak instead of type. On
  some devices/browsers this may be unavailable.

## Privacy at a glance

Your journal and check-ins are stored **locally** on your device by default. The
app does not track you across other apps. Verification and community features
are opt-in. See `PRIVACY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` for the full policy.

## If you're in crisis

If you're in immediate danger, use the **SOS** button or call your local
emergency number. The Help & Redress tab lists mental-health and crisis lines.
Aethon Beacon is a supportive tool, not a substitute for professional or
emergency care.
