Privacy Policy

Aethon Beacon  ·  Last updated: June 28, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

🔒 Short version: Everything you write stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your emotional check-ins, journal entries, and health information never leave your phone unless you explicitly export them yourself.

1. Who We Are

Aethon Beacon ("the app", "we", "our") is a personal wellness application designed to help individuals track emotional states, access guided wellness practices, and receive personalised insights. The app is operated by its developer and is available on iOS and Android.

For questions about this policy, contact us at: trikuta9081@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect — and Don't

Data stored only on your device (never transmitted to us):

  • Emotional check-in entries (mood, tone, journal text, tags)
  • Profile information you enter (name, identity role, date of birth)
  • Vedic birth chart data derived from your date of birth
  • Streak and habit data
  • Reminder and notification preferences
  • Trusted contact names and numbers (stored locally, never synced)
  • App usage preferences and settings

Data we do NOT collect:

  • We do not use analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • We do not run advertising networks
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or photo library
  • We do not create user accounts or store data on our servers

3. Local Storage

All data is stored using your device's secure local storage (AsyncStorage on React Native). This data is included in your standard iOS/Android device backup if you have iCloud or Google Backup enabled. You can disable this in your device settings.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data from your device.

4. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local push notifications on your device. These notifications are generated entirely on-device — no data is sent to our servers to schedule them. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the app.

5. Community Features

Aethon Beacon includes a peer community space. If you post in the community:

  • Posts are visible to other verified users of the community
  • Do not include personally identifying information in community posts
  • The community space is moderated for safety

Community feature infrastructure details will be disclosed fully when live peer networking is activated in a future release.

6. Emergency and Crisis Features

The app includes an emergency dial button and displays crisis helpline numbers (including iCall: 9152987821). Pressing the emergency dial button initiates a phone call using your device's standard calling app. We do not monitor or record these calls.

If you are in crisis, please contact a professional. This app is not a substitute for medical or psychological care.

7. Voice Features

If you enable voice guidance, text-to-speech is performed locally using your device's built-in speech engine (expo-speech / AVSpeechSynthesizer on iOS). No audio is transmitted to us.

8. Data Export

You can export your check-in data at any time via Settings → Export Backup. The exported file is saved to your device and shared using your device's standard share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

9. Children's Privacy

Aethon Beacon is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has entered personal data into the app, uninstalling the app will delete all data.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may link to external websites (mental health resources, helplines, government portals). These links open in your device browser or a system web view. Third-party sites have their own privacy policies which we do not control.

We use the following system-level frameworks that may have their own data practices:

  • Expo / React Native — app runtime framework. See expo.dev/privacy
  • Apple / Google Push — local notification delivery via OS. No content is transmitted.

11. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Export: Use Settings → Export Backup
  • Delete: Clear entries in Settings, or uninstall the app
  • Correction: Edit any entry or profile field at any time

For GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), or PDPB (India) enquiries, contact us at trikuta9081@gmail.com. Given that no personal data is transmitted to us, most regulatory obligations are satisfied by design.

12. Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security (device PIN, Face ID, fingerprint). We recommend keeping your device's OS and the app updated. We have no ability to access your data remotely because it is not stored on our servers.

13. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where required by law, notify you within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
