Aethon Beacon

Terms of Use

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Purpose

Aethon Beacon is a support and clarity tool. It is not a substitute for emergency services, licensed medical care, legal advice, counselling, or professional judgment.

Safety

If the user is in immediate danger, the app should not be the only response. Use SOS, emergency services, nearby authorities, trusted contacts, or qualified professionals first.

User Conduct

Users should not post adult, explicit, abusive, harassing, threatening, illegal, spam, or unsafe content. Community moderation may hide, block, report, or pause content that breaks these rules.

Local-First Behavior

The current release is local-first by default. Data stays on the device unless the user exports it or later opts into another storage model.

Community and Verified Access

Community chat and messaging are limited to users whose phone and email have both been verified. No moderation system is perfect, so users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

External Links

The app may open official government sites, helplines, professional directories, or other third-party pages. Those services are controlled by their own providers and their terms apply separately.

Changes

The app, its features, and these terms may change over time.
