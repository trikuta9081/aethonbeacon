Help test Aethon Beacon on Android

Aethon Beacon is in Google Play closed testing. Please opt in with your Gmail account, install the app, keep it installed, and send feedback. Your opt-in helps the app qualify for production access.

1. Join closed test 2. Install from Play Send feedback

Steps

  1. Open the join link using your Gmail account.
  2. Tap Become a tester or Join.
  3. Install Aethon Beacon from Google Play.
  4. Use the app and keep your tester opt-in active for 14 days.

What to test

  1. Open the app and check the Today screen.
  2. Try voice input/readout if available.
  3. Try message/beep/notification behaviour.
  4. Try Vedic/moon-chart guidance and community screens.

Feedback

Email screenshots, crashes, device model, Android version, and what felt confusing to:

slathiarimple567@gmail.com

Google requires at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before production access can be requested. Thank you for helping us cross that bridge.
