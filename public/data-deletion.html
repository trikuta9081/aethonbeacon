Delete Local App Data

Aethon Beacon is local-first. Most profile details, journal entries, check-ins, saved guidance, trusted contacts, and settings stay on the user's device. Users can delete this information by clearing the app's local data, uninstalling the app, or using reset/clear controls provided inside the app where available.

Request Deletion of Online Data

To request deletion of any online data connected with Aethon Beacon verification, Beacon Guide, or community safety support, email trikuta9081@gmail.com with the subject "Aethon Beacon data deletion request".

Please include:

  • the email address or phone number used for verification, if any
  • the approximate date of the verification, Beacon Guide, or community interaction
  • a short note saying which data you want deleted

What Can Be Deleted

We will delete or de-identify verification records, support requests, community safety notes, and related backend records that can reasonably be matched to the request.

What May Be Kept

Some limited records may be retained temporarily when needed for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or fraud prevention. Verification codes expire quickly and are not intended to be kept as permanent account records.
