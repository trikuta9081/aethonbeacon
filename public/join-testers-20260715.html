Android testers

  1. Tap Request access and submit the same Gmail you use on Google Play.
  2. Your Gmail is added by hand to a Play Console tester email list — usually within 24 hours.
  3. Once you get confirmation, tap Join closed test above.
  4. If Play still says “only invited people can download,” your Gmail hasn't been added yet — wait for confirmation, or resubmit the request below.
  5. After opt-in, install from Google Play and keep testing active for 14 days.

iPhone / TestFlight testers

  1. Open the web app now on iPhone while the native TestFlight invite is pending.
  2. Install Apple TestFlight from the App Store.
  3. Enter the Apple ID email you use on your iPhone in the form below.
  4. If you lost the invitation or TestFlight asks for a redeem code, submit the same Apple ID email again. The server will request a fresh Apple invitation when App Store Connect automation is available.
  5. If a public TestFlight link is not yet enabled, the Apple ID email must be invited from App Store Connect.
  6. After the invite arrives, open it on the iPhone, accept the beta, and install NAYIQ.
  7. Optional web fallback: in Safari, open nayiq.co and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  8. Send feedback/screenshots from TestFlight or from this page.

Controlled beta checklist

Complete this short release check before sending feedback. Do not include private counselling text, birth details, phone numbers, or message contents.

  1. Record device model, operating-system version, app build, theme, text size, and network quality.
  2. Check onboarding, Automatic Counselling, Vedic Insights, Calm Sound, Community, and Help and Redress.
  3. Confirm text remains readable on every screen and that no card, button, or page overlaps another.
  4. Check microphone, spoken reply, tone playback, notification sound, and vibration where supported.
  5. Report severity as: blocker, major, moderate, or minor; include exact reproduction steps and a redacted screenshot.

Required coverage: small and large Android phones, older and current supported iPhones, tablet, slow network, large accessibility text, light and dark themes, and at least one supported non-English language.

Release target: 30–50 active testers, no open blocker, and verified correction of every major issue before promotion.
